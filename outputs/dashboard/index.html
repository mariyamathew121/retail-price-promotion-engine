Trend sign flip is a warning

Model interpretation

PPML estimates range from −1.301 (FOODS_1) to −0.353 (HOUSEHOLD_2). Across-store results show material heterogeneity. Observational controls and fixed effects do not remove every time-varying confounder; treat these values as experiment-prioritization evidence.

Candidate quality note

The highest-lift pairs are mostly flavour variants of one product, so they are weak bundle candidates until validated.

Validation is not optional

A high lift ranks co-occurrence against chance under the sample; it does not show that promotions or price changes cause complementary demand.

!
No price data in Instacart

These candidates cannot estimate cross-price elasticity or bundle-induced incremental sales from the basket dataset alone.
