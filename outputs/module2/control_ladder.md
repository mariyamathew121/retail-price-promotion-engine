# Module 2 control ladder

Median OLS log-log elasticity over 100 selected item-store series (`log1p(units)` on `log(price)`, weekly, same series as the DML run).

| Controls | Median OLS elasticity |
|---|---:|
| no_controls | -0.043 |
| seasonality_snap_event | -0.054 |
| plus_promo_flag | -0.742 |
| plus_promo_flag_and_linear_trend | 1.145 |

`promo_flag` is derived from price (a drop of at least 10% below the prior eight-week maximum), so adding it removes much of the deep-discount variation from the treatment. The large move from the second to the third row therefore mixes confounding control with lost price variation, and the trend row shows how sensitive the result is to specification.
